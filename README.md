## Favorite Series or Movies
1. I'm Drunk, I Love You
   - This Filipino film portrays two college best friends who take an unexpected excursion to La Union shortly before graduation.  Carson hides her long-held unspoken love for Dio, who is completely unaware of her sentiments.  Carson must decide whether to ultimately confess or let go as they face the bittersweet truth of growing up and moving on, reflecting the sadness and humor of loving someone who may never return your love.
3. 18 Again
   -  It is a Korean fantasy romantic series that rewrites the rules for second chances. The story revolves around a 37-year-old man who is about to get divorced and somehow returns to his 18-year-old self while keeping his mature attitude. Living in his younger self, he secretly observes his family's problems and recognizes the sacrifices he previously neglected. Through his journey, he discovers that while youth fades, love, duty, and family are what actually make life meaningful.
5. Extraordinary Attorney Woo
   - This Korean series depicts a smart young lawyer with autism spectrum disorder who joins a prestigious law firm in Seoul.  She takes on complex problems with originality and emotion with help of her outstanding skills in recalling and a unique style of thinking.  Despite societal difficulties and discrimination, she demonstrates that knowledge, empathy, and integrity may push her to amazing heights, both within and outside of the courtroom.
